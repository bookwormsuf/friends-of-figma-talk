# Closing animation: the three variations

Kept for reference, 15 November 2026. Not built or tested. These are the files as they were when the closing animation had three endings (1 calm, 2 energetic, 3 playful), three size options, and the `?ending` and `?size` switches.

Sufyan picked ending 1 (calm) and tuned it with the dials. The live files in `src/slides` and `tests` now hold only that one. To bring back the energetic or playful motion, copy the `v===2` or `v===3` branches from `closingMotion.ts.txt`.
